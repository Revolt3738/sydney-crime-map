Methods and sources

Incident data were published by the NSW Bureau of Crime Statistics and Research (BOCSAR); populations are from the Australian Bureau of Statistics (ABS) 2021 Census. The weighted rate divides total incidents by total population across suburbs with calculable rates. Total assault includes domestic and non-domestic assault; violent crime also includes assaults on police, sexual offences and robbery. These assault data do not separate injuries or their severity.
